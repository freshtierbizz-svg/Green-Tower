# Department 1 — Strategy & Copy Deliberation Report (v001)

## 1. Strategy Lead Proposal

### Positioning & Brand Narrative
AeroHarvest is positioned as an **agricultural technology hardware system** for clean, home-grown produce. It rejects cheap dropshipping aesthetics, "get-rich-quick" urban farming gimmicks, and low-grade PVC knockoffs. The brand speaks to affluent homeowners, health-conscious families, and eco-tech enthusiasts who value design excellence, self-reliance, and uncompromised food purity.

### Core Value Hierarchy
1. **Purity & Health**: Zero pesticides, zero soil-borne pathogens, 100% nutrient-controlled produce.
2. **Design & Craftsmanship**: Sleek, silent, architectural aeroponic hardware designed for modern balconies and living spaces.
3. **Effortless Automation**: Automated watering timers and low-maintenance closed-loop circulation.
4. **Economic & Environmental Rationality**: 90-95% water reduction compared to traditional gardening; year-round harvests.

### Retail Pricing Recommendation & Justification
- **Base System**: **AUD $599** (inclusive of 10% GST).
- **Complete Kit**: **AUD $699** (includes starter nutrients, nursery seedling tray, pH kit, lockable caster base).
- **Justification**: Grounded directly in `docs/research/competitive_pricing_research_v001.md`. The AUD $159–$170 landed cost yields a ~73%–77% gross margin. This undercuts Airgarden ($799 AUD) by $100–$200 while offering a more modern aesthetic, and sits alongside Mr Stacky ($580–$680 AUD) with superior brand trust and materials.

### Conversion Architecture
- Hero Section: Immediate high-tech visual impact + explicit value proposition + Primary Shopify Buy CTA (`#shopify-buy-button-hero`).
- Trust Bar: 2-Year Australian Warranty (pending final policy), Free Metro Shipping, 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee.
- Interactive Explainer: How Vertical Aeroponics Mists Roots (clean technical diagrams).
- What's Included vs. What Customer Provides: 100% transparent component checklist to prevent post-purchase buyer friction.
- Competitor Differentiation Table: Honest comparison against soil gardening and cheap PVC drip towers.
- Final Purchase Area: Secondary Shopify Buy CTA (`#shopify-buy-button-final`) with clear payment options (Shop Pay, Afterpay, Credit Card).

## 2. Devil's Advocate / QC Challenge

### QC Reviewer Findings:
1. **Claim Control Audit**:
   - *Issue*: Initial draft marketing copy attempted to state "Harvest 10kg of fresh greens every month!"
   - *QC Action*: **REJECTED**. Violates the Claim Status Rule and Non-Negotiable Product Truth Rule. Registered in `PRODUCT_TRUTH.md` as `CLM-004` under status `Supplier Claim — Unverified`. Mandatory replacement wording in all drafts: `[PRODUCT_YIELD_TBD]`.
2. **Dropshipping Language Audit**:
   - *Issue*: Words like "revolutionize your life", "miracle grower", or "limited stock left!" detected in draft bullet points.
   - *QC Action*: **REJECTED**. Violates Business Reality Rule. Replaced with restrained, engineering-focused terminology ("Automated root misting", "Precision nutrient delivery", "Food-grade UV-stabilized polymer").
3. **Competitive Research Verification**:
   - *QC Action*: Verified that competitive research captured all required fields across 5 Australian competitor tiers (Airgarden, Mr Stacky Air Stacky, Mr Stacky Green Stack, Urban Green Farms, eBay imports) with explicit URLs and access dates (2026-09-07).
