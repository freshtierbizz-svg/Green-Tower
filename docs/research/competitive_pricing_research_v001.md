# Australian Vertical Aeroponics & Hydroponics Competitive Pricing Research (v001)

## Executive Summary
This research benchmarks real Australian competitors in vertical farming, aeroponic towers, and hydroponic systems against AeroHarvest's landed cost of **AUD $159–$170**. The market is divided into budget unbranded DIY stacks ($100–$350 AUD), mid-tier consumer towers ($450–$680 AUD), premium residential aeroponic towers ($799 AUD), and commercial powerhouse towers ($2,000–$3,000+ AUD).

## Commercial Price Recommendation & Justification

### Recommended Retail Price (RRP):
- **Base AeroHarvest Aeroponic Tower System**: **AUD $599** (inclusive of 10% GST).
- **AeroHarvest Complete Growing Kit** (Tower + Starter Nutrients + Seedling Nursery Tray + Caster Base): **AUD $699** (inclusive of 10% GST).

### "Why This Price?" Justification:
1. **Landed Cost & Gross Margin**:
   - Landed Cost: **AUD $159–$170**.
   - Gross Profit at AUD $599 Base: **AUD $429–$440** per unit (~73% Gross Margin).
   - Gross Profit at AUD $699 Kit: **AUD $529–$540** per unit (~77% Gross Margin).
   - This margin structure provides necessary capital for Australian customer acquisition (CAC), localized customer service, 2-year warranty reserve, free metro AU shipping incentives, and sustainable scaling.

2. **Competitive Market Positioning**:
   - Undercuts **Airgarden** ($799 AUD) by **$100–$200 AUD**, instantly creating a price advantage for price-sensitive Australian homeowners comparing premium aeroponic systems.
   - Sits directly alongside mid-range modular competitors (**Mr Stacky Air Stacky** at $580–$680 AUD), but wins on superior visual aesthetics, premium anti-template brand identity, food-grade materials, and modern digital customer support.
   - Positions comfortably above cheap PVC knockoffs ($120–$350 AUD), preserving high-tech agricultural credibility without alienating mainstream buyers.
